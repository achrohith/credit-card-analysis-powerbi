## Project Overview
This project analyses where a credit card portfolio earns its revenue and which customers and spending categories drive it. The first page covers transactions, card types and spending categories. The second page covers customer profiles such as occupation, age, income, marital status and location.

## Business Objectives
- Measure total revenue, interest earned and transaction volume.
- Compare performance across card types (Blue, Silver, Gold and Platinum).
- Identify the expense categories and payment methods that generate the most revenue.
- Profile the customers who contribute the most revenue.
- Track revenue trends by quarter and by week.

## Report Features
- **KPI cards:** Revenue, Interest Earned, Transaction Amount, Transaction Count, Income and Satisfaction Score.
- **Slicers:** Quarter, Card type, Gender and Week start date filter every visual on the page.
- **Transaction page:** Card-level summary table, quarterly revenue with transaction trend, and revenue by chip usage, expense category, education level, occupation and card type.
- **Customer page:** Weekly revenue trend, revenue and income by job, and revenue by age group, marital status, income group and top 5 states.

## Key Insights

### Overall Performance
- Total revenue is **$55.3M** with **$7.8M** in interest earned, from **655.7K** transactions worth **$44.5M**.

### Card Types
- **Blue** cards generate **$46.1M**, about **83%** of total revenue.
- **Silver** adds $5.6M (10%), **Gold** $2.5M (4%) and **Platinum** only $1.1M (2%).

### Spending Behaviour
- **Swipe** transactions bring in **$35M** (about 63% of revenue), well ahead of Chip ($17M) and Online ($3M).
- **Bills** is the top expense category ($14M), followed by Entertainment ($10M), Fuel ($9M) and Grocery ($9M).

### Customer Profile
- **Businessmen** contribute **$17.4M** (about 31%), and together with White-collar customers ($10.1M) they generate about half of all revenue.
- **Graduates** are the highest revenue education group ($22M), followed by High School ($11M).
- The **40-50** age group is the largest (about $25M), followed by **50-60** (about $19M).
- **High income** customers contribute about **$29M**, more than half of total revenue.
- **Married** customers (about $28M) generate more revenue than **Single** customers (about $23M).
- **Texas, New York and California** are the top states, each contributing about $12-13M.

### Trends
- Quarterly revenue is stable between **$13.3M and $14.2M**, with **Q3** the highest and **Q4** the lowest.
- Transactions peak in Q3 (**166.6K**) and fall to **161.6K** in Q4.
- Weekly revenue is volatile, moving between roughly **$0.4M and $0.8M**.
- The average satisfaction score is **3.19**, which leaves room for improvement.

## Recommendations
1. **Grow premium cards.** Gold and Platinum make up only about 6% of revenue, so target high-income customers with upgrade offers.
2. **Increase chip and online usage.** Online is only about 5% of revenue, so rewards or cashback could grow it.
3. **Focus on high-value segments.** Businessmen, graduates and high-income customers drive most revenue, so design retention offers for them.
4. **Address the Q4 decline.** Run seasonal campaigns to lift spending and transactions in the final quarter.
5. **Improve customer satisfaction.** A score of 3.19 suggests service gaps, so analyse the drivers by card type and segment.
